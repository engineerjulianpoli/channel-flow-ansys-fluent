# Channel Flow Simulation with High-Temperature Injection

Computational fluid dynamics project completed at The City College of New York using ANSYS Fluent to analyze flow behavior within a rectangular channel containing an orthogonal injection stream.

## Overview
The project investigated how changes in inlet and injection velocities affect flow patterns, pressure fields, velocity distributions, mixing behavior, and vorticity within the channel.

Two flow cases were modeled in ANSYS Fluent and compared using streamline, velocity, pressure, and vorticity results.

## Tools
- ANSYS Fluent
- Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)
- Fluid Mechanics
- Continuity Equation
- Linear Momentum Equation

## Analysis Performed
- Generated pathlines and streamline vectors
- Evaluated velocity contours
- Evaluated pressure contours
- Analyzed vorticity behavior
- Compared flow behavior under different inlet conditions
- Verified mass conservation using the continuity equation
- Evaluated momentum balance using pressure and force results

## Key Findings
Higher inlet and injection velocities produced stronger jet penetration, increased mixing, larger velocity gradients, and more pronounced pressure variations within the channel.

Mass conservation and momentum balance checks were used to verify that the simulation results were consistent with fundamental fluid mechanics principles.
